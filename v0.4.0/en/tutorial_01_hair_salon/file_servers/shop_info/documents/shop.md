# About Willow Hair Studio

## Hours and location
- Open every day from 9 AM to 7 PM. Each designer has their own days off.
- 214 Main Street, Suite 2 (an example address). Free parking in the lot behind the building; street parking is metered.

## Booking and cancellation
- Book by chat or by phone.
- Please change or cancel at least 24 hours ahead. A late cancellation or a no-show may be charged 50% of the service.
- If you are more than 15 minutes late, we may need to shorten your service or move you to another time.

## Payment
- All major credit cards, Apple Pay, Google Pay and cash.
- Prices do not include tip. You can add a tip for your designer at checkout.
