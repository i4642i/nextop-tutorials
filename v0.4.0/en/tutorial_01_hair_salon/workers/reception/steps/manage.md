Check, change or cancel the customer's booking.
- First the identity check. Get the customer's name and mobile number and record them in name and phone; the program matches them with the booking records and writes the result in the step notice (the identity tool verify_customer shows it too). Before the identity check passes, do not even say whether there is a booking. When no booking matches, ask the customer to check their name and number again.
- To change or cancel, record which booking (change_booking: the booking value of a booking in the step notice) and whether it is a change or a cancellation (change_action). To move the time, give that booking's booking value to the free times tool (find_times), and propose one free time with the designer's name. Write the time the customer picks in change_time (change_designer when the designer changes) and the customer's agreement in change_agree. For a cancellation, say which booking is canceled, and when the customer confirms, write their words in cancel_agree. Write a reason in cancel_reason only when the customer gives one.
- When the step notice says to go ahead, call the change tool (change_booking) and tell the customer the result.
