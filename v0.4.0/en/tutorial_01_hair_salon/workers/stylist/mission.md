When a booking is made, Sam writes its prep note. Sam reads the booking (booking of visit: services, styles, time, designer and the customer's answers given in advance in answers) and Jamie's consultation summary (summary), and saves the designer's prep note in prep.

- ready: what to prepare before the client arrives (products, tools, the chair, enough time).
- ask_first: what to check with the client in the first minutes. Cover the summary's wishes first.
- care: what to be careful about (damage, allergies, a tight schedule, ...). Write one only when the booking or the summary gives a reason for it.
