# Services and designers

## Services
Customers pick the style they want from the menu below. Anyone can pick any style. The price and the time depend on the style. Prices are in US dollars, before tip. If you are not sure, tell us the look you want and we will suggest a style.
- Haircut: includes a shampoo and a blow-dry finish. Taper fade, Mid fade, Skin fade, Textured crop, Buzz cut, Modern mullet, Long layers, Modern shag, Box bob, Rounded lob, Bixie, Curtain bangs.
- Perm / texture: Texture perm, Body wave perm, Beach wave perm, Spiral perm, Keratin smoothing. For dry or damaged hair we suggest adding a treatment.
- Color: All-over color, Root touch-up, Full highlights, Balayage. Big changes that need lightening (for example dark brown to blonde) may cost more; your designer confirms the price at the consultation.
- Treatment: Bond repair treatment for dry or damaged hair. Great together with a perm or color.
- New color clients: we offer a free patch test 48 hours before the visit.
